Transcript folding

The same Queen session rendered as it is today and as it would be with turn folding, a bookkeeping tier, coalesced thoughts and mail, and a live "now" line. A Codex worker follows so the treatment is checked against a coding session, not only orchestration.

The principle behind every change: a live turn shows the machinery; a finished turn shows the outcome. You watch it work; you scroll past what it did. Nothing is discarded — every folded line opens on click, and ctrl+o still reveals everything, including the bookkeeping rows that no longer appear in history at all.

The panes below are interactive: click a ▸ line to unfold it, and click ctrl+o details in a pane's footer to turn the bookkeeping tier back on.

Why it happens today

  • The transcript is a flat, append-only event log; turn-idle only stamps timestamps (view-state.ts:1791).
  • There is one density and it is already the minimum — ctrl+o only goes up (transcript-view.ts:148).
  • The view cannot tell a wake turn from a user turn, though dispatchedWakes knows (agent-ui.ts:2009).
  • toolKind has no bookkeeping tier; mcp__hive__* renders like Run.
  • Thoughts fragment per tool call; mail notices append per notice.

What changes

  • A · Bookkeeping tier: shown live, hidden in history, back with ctrl+o.
  • B · One duration per turn, carried on the fold line; no Worked for rows.
  • C · Mail notices coalesce per lane and live in the footer's mail phase.
  • D · Turn folding on turn-idle, keyed by the existing turnId.
  • E · Wake origin on the turn; adjacent wake turns fold together; narration folds inside.
  • F · Footer names the step in flight.

Order of work

  1. A + B + C first. Small, independent, and alone they remove about 70% of the rows in the session above.
  2. D + E together — they share the turn record. This is the structural fix and where the click-to-open behaviour lands.
  3. F last; it is a footer change and only matters once history stops carrying the liveness signal.

Not proposed: a third density level. Two states — folded history with click-to-open, and ctrl+o for everything — match what people already know from the vendor CLIs and keep one toggle in the footer.
